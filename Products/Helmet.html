AGV Pista GP RR

The AGV Pista GP RR is a high-performance motorcycle helmet designed for racing and extreme riding conditions.
It features a lightweight carbon fiber shell, advanced safety certifications, and cutting-edge technology for optimal protection and comfort.

Product Specifications

Specification Details
Shell Material: 100% Carbon Fiber
Shell Sizes: 4 distinct shell sizes
EPS (Inner Shell): 5-density EPS developed in 4 sizes
Weight: Approximately 1,450g (in the smallest shell size)
Homologation/Safety: FIM Racing Homologation, ECE 22.06, DOT
Ventilation System: 5 front metal air vents, 2 rear metal extractors
Aerodynamics: Detachable PRO Spoiler; designed to blend with race suits
Visor Thickness: 5mm (Optic Class 1)
Field of View: 190° horizontal, 85° vertical
Visor Features: Anti-scratch, tear-off ready, 120 Max Vision Pinlock included
Retention System: Titanium Double-D Ring
Interior Lining: Shalimar fabric (cheek pads), microfiber (crown pad); fully removable/washable
Hydration System: Integrated drinking system included
Fitment: 360° Adaptive fit, no stitches in sensitive areas
