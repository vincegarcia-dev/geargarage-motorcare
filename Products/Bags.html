MOTOWOLF MDL0718

The MOTOWOLF MDL0718 is a versatile leg bag designed for motorcycle riders who need reliable storage without compromising on style.

It features a durable hard shell construction, expandable compartments, and an ergonomic height-adjustable fit.

Product Specifications

Specification Details
Materials Built with a protective PU hard shell and durable 420D nylon fabric.
Dimensions 27 cm (Height) x 17 cm (Width) x 7 cm (Thickness).
Expandability The thickness can be expanded by 5 cm (up to 12 cm total) to store bulkier items like phones or wallets.
Water Resistance The outer materials are waterproof and water-resistant, keeping your belongings safe from the elements.
Fit & Comfort Features an ergonomic design that is height-adjustable and secures with a quick-release buckle for easy access.
Tech Features Includes an integrated earphone port, allowing you to route cables without unzipping the bag.
