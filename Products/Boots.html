SHIMA Strato

The SHIMA Strato is a lightweight, mid-height men's motorcycle boot built for adventure, touring, and urban commuting.
It features an innovative ATOP lacing system for easy adjustments, comprehensive SAP™ ankle protection,
and comes in both a highly ventilated standard version and a waterproof WP model.

Product Specifications

Specification / Feature Details
Riding Style Adventure, touring, and urban commuting; lightweight, mid-height design
Primary Materials Microfiber and breathable mesh
Closure System ATOP Reel Knob lacing system for one-handed tightening, plus a rear pulling strap
Weather Resistance Standard model is highly ventilated for summer/mid-season; Strato WP features a Teflon-coated NextDry™ waterproof, breathable membrane
Ankle Protection SAP™ (SHIMA Advanced Protection) TPU cover plate and Armor Plus impact-absorbing foam
Heel & Toe Safety Reinforced heel cup, hardened toe cap, and reinforced double safe seams against tearing
Sole Construction Ergonomic, cross-reinforced, oil-resistant, and non-slip rubber sole with embedded steel shank
Gear Control Rubberized front gear shift pad for increased lever grip and abrasion protection
Visibility Night Vision System utilizing large reflective glass microsphere panels for nighttime riding
